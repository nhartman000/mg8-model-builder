# NYCH Professional Domain Taxonomy — Expanded Draft

**Purpose:** a broad, consistently structured candidate taxonomy for NYCH domain discovery and later human-reviewed database population.

**Status:** proposed taxonomy expansion, not a canonical or exhaustive occupational standard. It expands all eight sectors and all 39 domain headings in the supplied reference. Specialty names describe areas of expertise; they do not assert licensure, competence, or authority to perform regulated work.

## How to read this outline

- **Sector** is the broadest grouping.
- **Domain** is a major field of practice or knowledge.
- **Subdomain** groups closely related specialties.
- **Specialty** is a distinct area of expertise or practice.
- Technologies, regulations, certifications, job titles, and procedures are examples or attributes, not necessarily taxonomy nodes.
- In NYCH/TOTE, reusable **functions** and their **techniques/TOTE loops** should attach to specialty nodes as separate records. A specialty is not itself a function or a loop.
- The same specialty may relate to multiple domains. Represent that relationship with explicit cross-links rather than copying the specialty as if it were unrelated.
